Research design recommendations

Use MAMI as the initial abundance-analysis cohort after QC. Keep PRIME as a metadata-only external cohort until its matching abundance matrix is acquired. Country is confounded with study/project, so use study-aware validation and avoid causal or generalizable country claims. Maternal age, BMI, gestational age and antibiotic exposure cannot currently be analysed reliably from the supplied metadata.
